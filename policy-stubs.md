## Shipping policy (stub)

**Title:** Shipping

Orders for {{BRAND}} are fulfilled via dropship from overseas suppliers unless a product page says otherwise.

- **Typical delivery window (overseas):** **15–30 days** after we place your order with the supplier, once payment has cleared.  
- **Tracking:** We send tracking when the carrier provides it. Early scan gaps are normal on overseas routes.  
- **Not Prime-speed:** We disclose ETAs up front so there are no surprises.  
- **Address accuracy:** You are responsible for a complete, deliverable address. Undeliverable parcels may not be recoverable.  
- **Customs / duties:** International destinations may face duties or import fees charged by your country — those are the buyer’s responsibility unless a product page says otherwise.  
- **Faster / domestic SKUs:** If a product ships from a domestic warehouse, that product page will show the confirmed window instead of 15–30 days.

## Refunds & returns (stub)

**Title:** Returns & refunds

We want the listing to match what arrives. Overseas dropship makes physical returns slow and sometimes impractical — please read this before ordering.

- **Damaged / wrong item / not as described:** Contact us within **7 days** of delivery with order number + photos. We will work a replacement or refund as appropriate after we verify with the supplier.  
- **Change of mind:** Because items ship from overseas, **change-of-mind returns are limited**. If we can accept a return, you may be responsible for return shipping and any supplier restocking rules. Ask before sending anything back.  
- **Refund timing:** Approved refunds go back to the original payment method; processor timing varies (often several business days).  
- **Cancelled before supplier ship:** If you cancel before we place the supplier order, we refund in full. After the supplier ships, cancellations follow the damaged/wrong-item path or are declined for change-of-mind.  
- **No guarantee of sales outcomes:** Product pages describe the item; they do not promise business results if you resell.

This stub is not legal advice. {{BRAND}} may update this page; the version on the site at purchase time applies.

Contact: {{SUPPORT_EMAIL_OR_FORM}}

## Privacy (stub)

**Title:** Privacy

{{BRAND}} collects information needed to run the store and fulfill orders.

- **What we collect:** Name, email, shipping address, order details, and basic analytics (e.g. pages viewed, UTM parameters on links you click). Payment card data is handled by the checkout provider (e.g. Shopify Payments / PayPal) — we do not store full card numbers on our own servers.  
- **Why:** To process orders, send tracking, answer support messages, and improve the site.  
- **Who we share with:** Fulfillment suppliers (shipping address + necessary order fields), checkout/payment processors, and hosting/store platforms. We do not sell your personal information.  
- **Cookies / analytics:** The store may use cookies or similar tech for session, cart, and basic analytics.  
- **Retention:** Order records kept as needed for accounting, fraud prevention, and legal requirements.  
- **Your requests:** Email {{SUPPORT_EMAIL_OR_FORM}} to ask for access, correction, or deletion where applicable.  
- **Kids:** Store not directed at children under 13 (or higher age required in your region).

This is a plain-language stub, not a complete GDPR/CCPA legal policy. Upgrade with counsel if you sell into regulated regions or process sensitive data.
